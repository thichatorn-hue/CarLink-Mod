Quick Setup & Name Refresh on iPhone

  1. Download and extract the release package, then install the APK on your device.
  2. Open the CarPlay menu, enter your desired vehicle name, and tap Save and reconnect.
  3. If iPhone still shows an older name, go to iPhone Settings > General > CarPlay, tap Forget This Car, and pair again.
